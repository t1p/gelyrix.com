FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to questions that usually come before a consultation.

Toby Hudson / Wikimedia Commons / CC BY-SA 3.0
Is it dangerous?

Risk depends on the scenario. We document risks, follow safety protocols, and disclose the experimental status.

Is it legal?

Status depends on jurisdiction and biomaterial type. Documents and applicable rules must be checked before a procedure.

Is revival realistic?

Human revival or identity restoration cannot be guaranteed today. The current goal is preserving structure and improving reversibility.

Why preserve animals?

Pets give owners a personal reason and help develop protocols with fewer legal complications than human cryonics.

How does the storage network work?

Local partners follow Gelyrix standards: protocols, launch support, monitoring, reporting, and coordination.

How is a cryomodule different from a regular building?

A cryomodule is designed around storage, safety, engineering systems, monitoring, and lab scalability.
