Investors and community

Funding concrete infrastructure and experiments

Support for Gelyrix should map to clear tasks: a study, a cryomodule, a regional location, or a public education material.

Funding formats

  • Donations and grants.
  • DAO or token participation where legally structured.
  • Funding specific experiments with result metrics.

Principles

  • Transparent budget and goals.
  • Open metrics and regular reports.
  • Ethical obligations to clients, participants, and future researchers.

Support slots

  • Laboratory research support.
  • Launch of new cryomodules.
  • Storage network expansion and local operator training.

DAO / GLRX disclaimer

GLRX is not an investment instrument. Community and DAO participation supports research and infrastructure without yield promises.
