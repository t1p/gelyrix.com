Lab

Open experiments instead of closed promises

The Gelyrix research program exists to test preservation quality, improve protocols, and publish understandable results.

Research areas

  • New cryoprotectants, including CSF-related media.
  • Perfusion protocols for tissues, organs, and neurosafekeeping.
  • Markers of structural and functional integrity.

Reporting format

  • Short reports after experiment stages.
  • Preprints and community posts.
  • A roadmap with planned tests and limitations.

How to join

  • Scientists and engineers can propose methods or equipment.
  • DAOs and investors can fund a specific experiment.
  • Partner storage sites and cryomodules connect to shared monitoring.
